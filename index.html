Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before applying.

Who can apply for a loan?
Anyone with an active EcoCash wallet, a valid Zimbabwean ID, and a mobile number that has been active for at least 6 months.
How fast will I receive my money?
Approved loans are disbursed to your EcoCash wallet within minutes. In rare cases, verification can take up to 24 hours.
What happens if I miss a payment?
A late fee applies and your loan limit may be reduced. We'll send reminders via SMS before your due date to help you stay on track.
Can I repay early?
Yes. Early repayment is always allowed with zero penalty. You only pay interest for the days you actually used the loan.
Is my personal data safe?
Your data is encrypted and never shared with third parties. We use bank-grade security standards and comply with Zimbabwe's data protection regulations.
